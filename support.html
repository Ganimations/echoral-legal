Before you write

  • Echoral does not require an account. Memories stay on your device.
  • Premium subscriptions are billed and cancelled through your Apple ID settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • Starting the 7-day free trial does not require payment information.
  • When a trial ends, your memories are not deleted.
  • Reinstalling the App does not restore memories. Use Settings → Backup & Restore and your .cebackup file.
