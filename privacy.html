Echoral

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Echoral (“the App”) handles information when you use the App on iPhone.

In this Policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Abdulgani Güngör, the developer and operator of Echoral, based in İzmir, Türkiye.

Echoral is designed as an on-device memory app. We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

2. Summary

  • Your memories are stored on your device.
  • We do not require an Echoral account.
  • We do not operate a cloud service that stores your memories for us.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App.
  • Some optional features send limited requests to Apple services (described below).

3. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored locally on your device:

  • Memory content you create, such as song titles, artist names, notes, tags, dates, and emotion labels.
  • Photos you choose to attach to memories.
  • Location details you choose to associate with a memory, if you grant Location permission.
  • App preferences and state, such as notification settings, onboarding completion, free-trial status, and local premium flags.
  • Local notification schedules you enable.

We do not receive automatic access to this on-device content. We would only see information you voluntarily send to us (for example, if you email us and attach files).

4. Permissions

Photos. Used only so you can select photos to attach to memories. The App does not upload your photo library to our servers.

Location (When In Use). Used only to help you pin memories to places and resolve place names when you choose those features. You may deny Location permission and continue using the App without location features.

Notifications. Used for local reminders and prompts you enable in the App. The App does not send remote push notifications from our servers.

5. Network requests and Apple services

Certain features require internet access and interact with Apple-operated services:

  • Song search and previews — search terms may be sent to Apple’s iTunes Search service.
  • Maps — map display and place lookups use Apple MapKit / Apple Maps.
  • Purchases — subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

6. Backups and exports

If you create a backup or export inside the App, the file is created on your device and remains under your control. Device-level backups (such as iCloud Backup) may include App data according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

7. Free trial and Premium

A free trial may be started and recorded locally on your device. Purchasing Premium is handled by Apple. Starting, ending, or purchasing Premium does not by itself delete your memories.

8. Retention and deletion

Your memories remain on your device until you delete them in the App, delete the App, or erase the device. Because we do not host your memories on our servers, we generally cannot remotely delete or restore on-device memories for you.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your jurisdiction.

10. Tracking and advertising

We do not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the App.

11. International use

On-device data remains on your device. If you use features that contact Apple services, processing may occur in locations where Apple operates those services.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date and make the updated Policy available here and in the App.
